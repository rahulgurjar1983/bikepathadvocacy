Bike paths in Bayside Council, New South Wales

By Rahul Gurjar, Kogarah

What the data shows

I checked 591.099 km (F1) of street that a bike may use. 135.891 km (F2) of it is safe for a child to ride alone. For 70.424 km (F3) I have no width.

I ask council to measure the street where I have no width. Please read the appendix to check every number.

How to check every number

F1: Street a bike may use

Value
591.099 km
Method
spec 03. I add up the length of every street segment inside the boundary where a bike may ride.
Files
  • segments.csv, sha256 9b64afe03d7061e1fdf25185f2a20e1f13b3e7845976ad8be91b0d26539c8df8
Sources
  • OpenStreetMap contributors, licence ODbL 1.0, request: network.osm.gz and boundary.geojson in the snapshot folder
Recipe: run it with python3 -c in a folder that holds the release files
import csv;print(round(sum(float(r['length_m']) for r in csv.DictReader(open('segments.csv')) if True)/1000,3))
